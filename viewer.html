How this was built

The PDF kept its AutoCAD layers, so the WALLS layer was rendered on its own, filled, and traced at a 50 mm grid. Scale comes from the 24010 mm frontage; the 20025 mm side then measures 20016, a 0.04% error.

Openings are wall gaps. Those the glazing layer touches get a sill and a head; the rest get a head only.

Check before using

North is contested. The title-block compass points at the edge the drawing labels EAST NEIGHBOR. The compass above follows the written boundary labels, not the arrow. The sun here is placed for legibility, not solar position.

Furniture, stairs, doors and glazing are drawn on other layers and are deliberately not modelled. This is the wall shell of two levels.
